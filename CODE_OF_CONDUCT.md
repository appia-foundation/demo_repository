# Code of Conduct

Appia Foundation spaces, including this repository, are for collaborative work. Everyone taking part is expected to help keep them constructive and welcoming.

## What we expect

- **Be respectful.** Disagree with ideas, not people.
- **Be constructive.** Explain your reasoning and suggest alternatives.
- **Assume good intent.** Ask for clarification before assuming a problem.
- **Be inclusive.** Not everyone has used GitHub before, and nobody should feel foolish for asking.
- **Respect confidentiality.** Don't share information that isn't yours to share, and don't post confidential material here.
- **Stay on topic.** Keep comments relevant to the issue or pull request at hand.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Deliberately disruptive, off-topic, or misleading contributions.
- Sharing other people's private information without permission.

## Reporting a concern

If you experience or see behaviour that breaks these expectations, please contact `<INSERT CONTACT NAME / EMAIL>`. Reports will be handled with discretion.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock conversations, or remove access to the repository where necessary to keep it a constructive space.

## Scope

This applies to all spaces connected to this repository: issues, pull requests, discussions, and comments.
