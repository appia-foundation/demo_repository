## 1. Scope

This document specifies requirements for the conformity assessment of example widgets and for the bodies that perform that assessment. It applies to widgets placed on the market by a provider, regardless of size or sector.

This document does not cover the design of widgets or the commercial terms under which they are supplied.

## 2. Normative references

The following documents are referred to in the text in such a way that some or all of their content constitutes requirements of this document.

- ISO/IEC 17000:2020, *Conformity assessment: Vocabulary and general principles*
- Appia - Architecture specifications

## 3. Terms and definitions

For the purposes of this document, the following terms and definitions apply.

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **widget** | An item that is the subject of conformity assessment under this document. |
| **provider** | A person or organisation that makes a widget available. |
| **conformity assessment** | Demonstration that specified requirements are fulfilled. |
| **assessment body** | A body that performs conformity assesment activities. |
| **assessor** | An individual who carries out conformity assessment on behalf of an assessment body. |
| **evidence** | Records or information that supports a claim of conformity. |

## 4. Requirements

### 4.1 Documentation

**REQ-4.1.1** The provider shall maintain a description of the widget, including its intended purpose.

**REQ-4.1.2** The provider shall retain evidence of conformity for at least five years after the widget is last made available.

### 4.2 Quality management

**REQ-4.2.1** The provider shall operate a documented process for handling reported defects.

**REQ-4.2.2** The provider should review that process at planned intervals.

### 4.2 Change control

**REQ-4.2.3** The provider shall record every change to the widget that could affect conformity.

The provider must make a Declaration of conformity available to the assessment body on request.

## 5. Conformity assessment

### 5.1 Assessment activities

**REQ-5.1.1** The assessment body shall review the evidence supplied by the provider.

**REQ-5.1.2** The assessor shall record the outcome of each assessment activity.

### 5.2 Outcome

**REQ-5.2.1** The assessment body shall issue a written report of its findings to the provider.

**REQ-5.2.2** Where a requirement is not met, the report shall identify the requirement concerned.
